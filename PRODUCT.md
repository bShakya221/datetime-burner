# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Vintage and film photography enthusiasts, content creators, and casual users who want to add authentic retro digital-camera timestamps to their photos. They value privacy (offline client-side processing), speed, and extreme visual authenticity.

## Product Purpose
RetroStamp PRO exists to burn authentic, pixel-crisp, retro digital-camera timestamps onto photos in the browser, without server uploads (ensuring privacy). Success is a tool that is highly usable and legible across both light and dark ambient lighting conditions.

## Brand Personality
Authentic, precise, tactile, and nostalgic.

## Anti-references
- Soft generic SaaS dashboard (pastel colors, rounded pill buttons, rounded cards).
- Low contrast text.
- Reinventing standard form controls.

## Design Principles
1. **Legibility & Contrast**: Ensure clear text contrast and visibility in both light and dark modes.
2. **Tactile Familiarity**: Standard form controls that are clean, easy to read, and consistent across themes.
3. **Frictionless Workflow**: Immediate feedback during setting adjustments and file processing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body and placeholder text in both themes.
- Accessible theme switching controls (header toggle).
